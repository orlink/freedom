Freedom Map

Can the people here make and carry out decisions about their own shared interests, directly or through accountable representatives?

Try:

Search for an address or click on the map. Each rectangle below is one level at which people share interests. Its colour shows how far the law lets them govern those interests themselves. Click a rectangle to see why: first direct versus representative decision-making, then the specific mechanisms, the properties each one needs, and the articles of law behind each answer.
